# LSP_REPO
1. Are late programming assignments accepted?

No. The syllabus states that all required work must be submitted by the due date and time, and no late assignments will be accepted.

2. Where will official course announcements be posted?

Official course announcements, resources, and assignment updates will be posted on Piazza. Students are expected to monitor Piazza regularly.

3. How will examination dates be announced?

The mid-term and final examination dates will be announced in class and posted on Piazza at least two weeks in advance. The syllabus currently lists the examination dates as TBD.

4. Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?

A makeup examination will be considered only under extraordinary circumstances beyond the student's control. 

5. Is AI permitted in this course?

Yes, but only when permitted by the instructor or the specific assignment.

6. Which course policy will have the greatest impact on your success this semester? Explain why.

The policy that will have the greatest impact on my success is the requirement to submit all assignments by the due date and time because no late assignments will be accepted and I am a procrastinator.

7. When are office hours for LSP Fall 2026?

Office hours are provided on an as-needed basis and by Zoom appointment.

8. What is your responsibility for monitoring Piazza, and are you responsible for assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted there?

Students are expected to monitor Piazza regularly for announcements, resources, and assignment updates. 

9. What documentation is required if you are unable to take an examination because of a medical issue or other approved circumstance?

The student must provide appropriate supporting documentation that verifies they were unable to participate in the examination at the scheduled time. 

10. Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements?

No it doesn't.
